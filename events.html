Something Is Always Happening

College life is more than classrooms and examinations. From cultural festivals and technical gatherings to entrepreneurship programmes and community initiatives, explore the experiences that bring the Sir MVIT campus together.

Major Cultural & Techno-Cultural Fests

Kalanjali – Techno-Cultural Festival

Organiser: Susamskriti Cultural Forum

Established: 1998

Format: Annual inter-collegiate festival

Kalanjali is one of Sir MVIT's flagship cultural celebrations. Started in 1998, this annual festival brings together students from colleges across Bengaluru and beyond to celebrate creativity, talent and innovation.

Festival Highlights

  • Dance and music competitions
  • Fine arts and creative exhibitions
  • Dramatics and theatre performances
  • Literary events and cultural activities
  • Technical showcases and student participation
Explore Kalanjali

Inceptum

Focus: Management, business and entrepreneurship

Inceptum brings students into the world of business strategy, management and creative problem-solving through departmental symposiums and business-centric activities.

Activity Areas

  • Corporate and management simulations
  • Marketing challenges
  • Business plan presentations
  • Team-based strategic activities

Flagship Technical Events & Hackathons

Hack-Nocturne

Organisers: TechHub Community and GLUG MVIT

Technical umbrella: Sutantra

Format: 24-hour hackathon

Hack-Nocturne is an intensive hackathon experience bringing together student developers, designers and innovators to transform ideas into working solutions.

Participants collaborate, experiment with emerging technologies and develop prototypes within a time-bound challenge.

Typical Focus Areas

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Network and Platform Security
  • Blockchain and Web3
  • Application and Web Development
  • Open Innovation
Visit Hack-Nocturne

Codefiesta

Focus: Programming and problem-solving

Codefiesta is a coding and problem-solving hackathon conducted at Sir MVIT. It challenges students to apply programming concepts, think logically and develop solutions to technical problems.

The documented 2022 edition was organised in association with ValuePitch e-Technologies.

Read Codefiesta Event Report

Smart India Hackathon – Internal Hackathon

Organiser: Institution's Innovation Council (IIC), Sir MVIT

The internal Smart India Hackathon is an institutional innovation event where student teams work on problem statements addressing real-world challenges.

It provides a platform for teams to present their ideas, develop prototypes and undergo institute-level screening for nomination to the national Smart India Hackathon.

Areas of Innovation

  • Healthcare and MedTech
  • Agriculture
  • Smart Automation
  • Disaster Management
  • Smart Education
  • Technology for social impact
Visit Smart India Hackathon

Institutional & Commemorative Events

Engineers' Day – Sir M. Visvesvaraya Birth Anniversary

Date: 15 September

Sir MVIT celebrates the birth anniversary of its namesake, Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya, every year on 15 September.

The occasion honours his contributions to engineering and nation-building. The celebration features invited speakers from literary, scientific and technical fields, inspiring students and faculty.

National Technology Day – Project Exhibition

Focus: Student innovation and engineering projects

Sir MVIT organises project exhibitions to commemorate National Technology Day. Undergraduate and postgraduate students get an opportunity to showcase their projects and engineering innovations.

Exhibition Highlights

  • Hardware and electronics prototypes
  • Software and application solutions
  • Engineering and research projects
  • Evaluation by industry and academic judges
  • Recognition and prizes for outstanding projects

Republic Day

Date: 26 January

Republic Day is commemorated on campus through flag-hoisting ceremonies and patriotic programmes involving students and staff.

Independence Day

Date: 15 August

The institute celebrates India's Independence Day with a campus flag-hoisting ceremony and programmes that honour the country's freedom and heritage.

Student Milestones & Annual Gatherings

Inaugural Function / Freshers' Day

Audience: Incoming first-year students and parents

The inaugural function welcomes newly admitted students into the Sir MVIT community. It provides students and parents an opportunity to interact with the Principal, Heads of Departments and the Training and Placement team.

College Day

Format: Annual institutional celebration

College Day marks the culmination of the academic year. The institute presents its annual reports covering academic, sports and cultural activities.

Students and faculty members are recognised for their accomplishments, with prizes distributed to winners across different domains.

Graduation & Farewell Day

Audience: Final-year students

A special send-off organised for graduating students to celebrate their college journey, achievements and memories before they begin their next chapter.

Entrepreneurship, Departmental & Community Events

Startup Mela

Organiser: E-Cell, Sir MVIT

Format: Two-day entrepreneurship event

Startup Mela connects student builders, startup founders, mentors and investors through business competitions, startup exhibitions, speaker sessions and networking opportunities.

Featured Competitions

  • Startup Survival Game: A business crisis-management simulation.
  • RebootX: A challenge focused on business recovery and strategic pivoting.
  • The Investor's Dilemma: A virtual investment and portfolio-management challenge.
  • IPL Auction: A strategic bidding and resource-allocation simulation.

Additional Experiences

  • Startup exhibitions
  • Mentor and investor connect
  • Founder speaker sessions
  • Industry panels and discussions
Explore Startup Mela

Departmental Technical Symposia

Organisers: Individual departments and student associations

Department-level technical events provide students with opportunities to explore their fields, exchange knowledge and demonstrate practical skills.

Common Activities

  • Technical paper presentations
  • Debugging and coding events
  • IoT and hardware exhibitions
  • Technical quizzes
  • Project demonstrations
  • Alumni and industry talks

Events and symposiums may be organised by departments including CSE, ISE, ECE, EEE, Civil, Mechanical, MCA and MBA.
