Hackathons & Innovation Challenges

Hack-Nocturne

Organisers: TechHub Community & GLUG MVIT

Technical umbrella: Sutantra

Format: 24-hour national/inter-collegiate hackathon

Hack-Nocturne is a flagship hackathon at Sir MVIT that brings together developers, designers and innovators to build creative technology solutions within an intensive 24-hour sprint.

Tracks

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Network and Platform Security
  • Blockchain and Web3
  • Application and Web Development
  • Open Innovation

Participants get opportunities to learn new technologies, collaborate with other developers, receive mentorship and present their solutions.

Explore Hack-Nocturne

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) – Internal Hackathon

Organiser: Institution's Innovation Council (IIC), Sir MVIT

Format: Internal innovation and selection competition

The internal Smart India Hackathon competition provides students with an opportunity to develop solutions for real-world problem statements released by government departments and industries.

It acts as an institute-level screening platform to identify and nominate student teams for the national-level Smart India Hackathon.

Problem Areas

  • Healthcare and MedTech
  • Smart Automation
  • Agriculture and Rural Development
  • Disaster Management
  • Smart Education
  • Other societal and industrial challenges

Students work in teams to understand a problem, brainstorm ideas, develop prototypes and pitch their proposed solutions.

Visit Smart India Hackathon

Codefiesta

Organiser: Sir MVIT in association with ValuePitch e-Technologies

Format: Coding and problem-solving hackathon

Codefiesta is a competitive programming and problem-solving hackathon conducted at Sir MVIT. It challenges participants to apply their programming knowledge, develop solutions and demonstrate technical productivity.

The 2022 edition was conducted online on 16 July 2022 and hosted on the Unstop platform.

The event included industry collaboration, sponsored prizes and problem statements designed to test participants' technical and problem-solving abilities.

Entrepreneurship & Business Challenges

Startup Mela

Organiser: E-Cell, Sir MVIT

Format: Two-day entrepreneurship and business event

Startup Mela is an entrepreneurship platform that brings together student innovators, early-stage founders, mentors and investors. It provides opportunities to experience business strategy, startup management, networking and innovation beyond the classroom.

Core Competitions

  • Startup Survival Game: A crisis-management simulation where teams navigate difficult business situations and market challenges.
  • RebootX: A strategic challenge focused on analysing a failed business model and developing a fresh growth strategy.
  • The Investor's Dilemma: A virtual investment challenge involving the allocation of a ₹100 crore corpus to build a portfolio.
  • IPL Auction: A bidding simulation that tests valuation, budgeting and resource-management skills.

More Than Competitions

  • Startup Exhibition: Explore emerging startups, products and business ideas.
  • Mentor Connect: Interact with industry mentors, founders and investors.
  • Investor Sessions: Learn about funding, investment and startup growth.
  • Speaker Sessions & Panels: Hear from entrepreneurs, corporate leaders and domain experts about building and scaling businesses.

Startup Mela is open to students from different colleges. Participants can compete, showcase ideas and connect with the startup ecosystem.
