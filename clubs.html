College is more than classrooms and examinations. It's about discovering your interests, meeting people and building something together.

Whether you're passionate about technology, arts, sports, culture or social service, there's a world of opportunities to explore at Sir MVIT.

🎭 Cultural, Dance & Theatre

Explore music, dance, theatre and cultural activities through the college's cultural communities.

Susamskriti Cultural Forum

Susamskriti provides a platform for students to explore creative arts and participate in cultural activities throughout the year.

  • Sunaada — Music
  • Laasya — Dance
  • Kalakriti — Fine Arts
  • Rangataranga — Theatre
  • Hongirana — Kannada Culture
  • Yaavani — English Forum
  • Vidya Bharathi — Indian Knowledge Systems
  • Sambhrama — Institutional Activities
  • SPIC MACAY — Indian Classical Arts and Culture

Major activities: VERVE, the annual talent-search competition, and KALANJALI, the inter-collegiate techno-cultural festival.
